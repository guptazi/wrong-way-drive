💡 Import Tips

  • Use YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:00 format for dateTime
  • Severity must be: Fatal, Injury, PDO, or Near-Miss
  • rampType: direct-connector, exit-ramp, entrance-ramp, loop-ramp
  • alcoholInvolved: Yes or No
  • Separate multiple countermeasures with | (pipe)
  • Lat/Lng must be in decimal degrees (e.g., 38.7551)
